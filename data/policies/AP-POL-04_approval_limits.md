# AP-POL-04 Invoice approval limits (delegation of authority)

## §2.1 Over-limit invoices
Invoices with a total amount above EUR 25,000 require approval by the budget owner in the approval workflow before payment, even when the invoice matches the PO. Until approved, the invoice is on hold with code WORKFLOW. AP routes or reminds the approver; AP cannot approve on the approver's behalf.

## §2.2 Below the limit
Invoices up to EUR 25,000 that match a PO need no further approval.

## §2.3 Escalation
If an approval has been pending for more than 5 working days, AP escalates to the approver's line manager.
