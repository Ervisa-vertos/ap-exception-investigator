# AP-POL-06 Vendor holds

## §2.1 Vendor on hold
When the vendor record itself is on hold (for example expired compliance documents, a quality review or pending sanctions screening), no invoice from that vendor may be paid. AP does not release individual invoices; it contacts vendor management, which owns the vendor hold, and the invoices stay on hold until vendor management lifts it.

## §2.2 Communication
AP may tell the vendor that a compliance or master-data topic is open, but must not disclose sanctions screening details.
