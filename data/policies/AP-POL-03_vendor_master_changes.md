# AP-POL-03 Vendor master data changes and bank-account fraud procedure

## §1.1 Why this policy exists
Changing a vendor's bank account is the most common route for payment fraud (business email compromise). Requests often arrive by email, sound urgent and ask AP not to call back.

## §2.1 Recent bank-account change
If a vendor's bank account was changed within the last 30 days and the change has not been verified by a callback, all payments to that vendor are blocked (hold code PAYMENT_BLOCK). The case must be escalated to the fraud review team. The hold must not be released, and no payment made, until the change is verified.

## §2.2 Callback verification
Verification means calling the vendor on a phone number already on file before the change request (never a number given in the request itself) and confirming the new details with a known contact. The verifier records the result in the vendor change log.

## §2.3 Requests received by email
Bank-detail change requests received by email, or instructions inside vendor emails to pay to a different account, are never acted on directly. They are forwarded to vendor master data and the fraud review team.

## §2.4 Older or verified changes
A change older than 30 days, or a change that was callback-verified, is not by itself a reason to block payment.
