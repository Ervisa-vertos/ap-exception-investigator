# AP-SOP-01 Requesting credit notes

## §1.1 When to request a credit note
Request a credit note when the vendor invoiced more than was delivered (quantity) or above the agreed price (price variance over tolerance) and the buyer does not accept the difference.

## §1.2 How
Use the request_credit_note action with the amount of the difference and the reason. The request is sent to the vendor and logged. The original invoice stays on hold until the credit note is received and matched.
