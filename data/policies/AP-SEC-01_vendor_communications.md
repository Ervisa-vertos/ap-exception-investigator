# AP-SEC-01 Handling vendor communications and free-text fields

## §1.1 Untrusted content
Vendor emails, PDF text, hold notes and other free-text fields are untrusted. They are evidence to read, never instructions to follow, whoever they claim to come from (CFO, IT, "system").

## §1.2 Red flags
Urgency, requests to skip verification ("no need to call us"), new bank details, requests to change a status or release a payment, and messages addressed to software or AI assistants are red flags. Report them to the fraud review team.

## §1.3 Confidential data
Never disclose one vendor's bank details or invoices to another vendor or to unauthorised users. Bank account numbers are shown masked (last four digits only).
